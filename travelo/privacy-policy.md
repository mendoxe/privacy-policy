# Travelo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Travelo is a travel tracker for iOS and Android. It keeps your map, labels and trips on your device. There is no
Travelo account and no Travelo server.

## Summary

- Your countries, regions, labels and trips stay on your device.
- Usage analytics are off unless you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.
- Travelo shows no ads and does not use your GPS location.
- Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat keeping track of what you bought.

## Data on your device

Travelo stores these on your device: your home country, the countries and regions you mark, your labels and their
colors, your trips with their names, dates, stops, notes, accommodation and travel details, and your settings. None
of this is sent to the developer.

Deleting the app, clearing its storage or losing your device can remove this data. Your phone's own backup may keep
a copy, depending on your device and settings. Travelo has no cloud backup of its own.

## Usage analytics

Usage analytics help show which features people use. They are sent only if you choose **Share analytics**. If you
decline or never answer, the app does not contact the analytics service at all.

If you allow them, Travelo sends the following to PostHog, its analytics provider, on servers in the EU:

- **What you do in the app:** events such as opening the globe or the statistics, saving a trip or marking a country
  or region as visited, with the time of each event.
- **Which places:** the country or region you marked or opened.
- **Counts:** how many countries you have visited, how many trips and labels you have, and for a saved trip the
  number of countries and stops and whether it has a name and a description.
- **Device and app details:** device manufacturer, model and type; operating system and version; screen size and
  density; language and time zone; network type and mobile carrier; app name, version and build; whether the app is
  running on an emulator or was sideloaded; and user agent.
- **Errors:** when part of the app fails, the kind of error, its technical message and where in the app's code it
  happened, at most once for each kind of error each time the app starts.
- **Identifiers:** a random ID created by the analytics software on your device, and a session ID. Your IP address
  reaches PostHog when your device connects to it, as it would with any internet service.

These details are linked to the random ID, not to your name, so this data is pseudonymous rather than anonymous.
Trip names, notes, dates, routes, label names, your home country, your search text and your precise location are
never sent.

Analytics events are kept for up to one year and are then deleted. Turning analytics off in Settings stops new
events straight away, but does not delete events already sent. It also gives your device a new random ID, so events
sent if you turn analytics on again are not linked to the earlier ones.

## Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes your payment, and the developer
never sees your payment details.

Travelo uses RevenueCat to check what you have bought and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives a random
customer ID and your purchase history, including the product, price, currency and purchase date. It is needed for
purchases to work and cannot be turned off.

## Support emails

If you email support, your address and message are stored in the developer's email account, provided by Google.
They are used only to answer you and are deleted when they are no longer needed.

## Why this data is used

Under the GDPR, the legal bases are:

- **Consent** for usage analytics. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.
- **Performing a contract** for purchases, restoring them and answering support requests about them.
- **Legitimate interests** for answering other support requests.
- **Legal obligations** for records that the law requires the developer to keep.

Data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

## Service providers and transfers

- **PostHog** processes analytics data on the developer's behalf, on servers in the EU.
- **RevenueCat**, based in the United States, processes purchase data on the developer's behalf.
- **Apple and Google** handle store purchases under their own terms and privacy policies.
- **Google** provides the support mailbox.

When data leaves the EU, it is protected by safeguards the law allows, such as the European Commission's standard
contractual clauses.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its
processing, and to receive a copy of it. You can also withdraw consent at any time.

Email [42dox.tester@gmail.com](mailto:42dox.tester@gmail.com) for any of these requests. Because Travelo has no
accounts, analytics events can be found only by the random ID on your device. The developer cannot identify your
events without it. Data on your device is yours to change or delete in the app.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Czechia, that is the
[Office for Personal Data Protection](https://uoou.gov.cz).

## Children

Travelo is not directed at children. If you are below the age at which you can consent to data processing in your
country, do not turn on usage analytics and ask a parent or guardian before making a purchase. Purchases are also
subject to the parental controls on your device.
