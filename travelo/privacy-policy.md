# Travelo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Travelo is a travel tracker for iOS and Android. There is no Travelo account and no Travelo server.

## Who is responsible

Travelo is made by an individual developer who publishes as mendox. The developer is responsible for the
processing described here and can be reached at 42dox.tester@gmail.com.

## Data on your device

Travelo stores your home country, the countries and regions you mark, your labels, your trips with their details,
and your settings on your device. None of this is sent to the developer.

## Usage analytics

Usage analytics are sent only if you choose **Share analytics**, and you can turn them off at any time in
**Settings → Share usage analytics**. They help show which features people use.

If you allow them, Travelo sends PostHog, its analytics provider, on servers in the EU: events in the app with their
time and the countries or regions they concern; counts of your countries, trips and labels; device and app details;
reports of errors in the app; a random ID created on your device; a session ID; and your IP address, which reaches
PostHog when your device connects to it.

These details are linked to the random ID, not to your name. Analytics events are kept for up to one year and are
then deleted.

## Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, which process your payment. Travelo uses RevenueCat to
check what you have bought and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives a random customer ID and your purchase
history, including the product, price, currency and purchase date.

## Support emails

If you email support, your address and message are stored in the developer's email account, provided by Google, and
are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

## Why this data is used

Under the GDPR, the legal bases are:

- **Consent** for usage analytics.
- **Performing a contract** for purchases, restoring them and answering support requests about them.
- **Legitimate interests** in answering other support requests.
- **Legal obligations** for records that the law requires the developer to keep.

Analytics are optional. Purchase data is needed to buy and restore purchases.

## Service providers and transfers

- **PostHog** processes analytics data on the developer's behalf, on servers in the EU.
- **RevenueCat**, based in the United States, processes purchase data on the developer's behalf.
- **Apple and Google** handle store purchases under their own terms and privacy policies.
- **Google** provides the support mailbox.

When data leaves the EU, it is protected by safeguards the law allows, such as the European Commission's standard
contractual clauses.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its
processing, and to receive a copy of it. You can withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect processing
before it. Email 42dox.tester@gmail.com for any of these requests.

Because Travelo has no accounts, analytics events can be found only by the random ID on your device, and the
developer cannot identify your events without it.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Czechia, that is the Office for Personal Data
Protection (https://uoou.gov.cz).

## Children

Travelo is not directed at children.
