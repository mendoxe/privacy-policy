# Travelo Support

Email [42dox.tester@gmail.com](mailto:42dox.tester@gmail.com) with your device, your app version (at the bottom of
**Settings**) and what happened.

## Questions

**Does Travelo work offline?**
Yes. The map, globe, regions, statistics and trips all work without an internet connection.

**Where is my data?**
On your device. Travelo has no account and no cloud storage.

**How do I move to a new phone?**
Use your phone's own backup and transfer. Travelo has no backup of its own, so make sure your phone's backup is on
before you switch.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Open the purchase screen and tap **Restore Purchases**, using the same Apple or Google account you bought with. If
it still does not unlock, email us the **Purchase ID** from the bottom of Settings; tapping it copies it.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by the store: [Apple](https://support.apple.com/118223) or
[Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

**How do I turn off usage analytics?**
Open **Settings → Share usage analytics**. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) for what they include.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device, including from your phone's backups if you delete those
too. For other requests, see [Your rights](privacy-policy.md#your-rights) in the privacy policy.
