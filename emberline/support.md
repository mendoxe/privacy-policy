# Emberline support

Support contact: **[42dox.tester@gmail.com](mailto:42dox.tester@gmail.com)**

For a problem or suggestion, include your device model, operating-system version, Emberline version shown in Settings, map/wave and what happened. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details or private account keys.

**Restore a purchase:** open Settings → Restore purchases while connected, using the same Apple/Google store account that purchased the content. Apple purchases do not automatically restore on Android or vice versa. Restoration recovers ownership; it does not restore local campaign saves.

**Offline play:** available maps and saved runs work offline. Purchasing, restoring and connecting a game account need a connection. Previously verified permanent content remains available during temporary outages.

**Game accounts:** optionally connect Google Play Games or Apple Game Center from Settings or the medals view. Local medals remain on your device; this initial release does not upload them as platform achievements or enable cloud saves. Connection failure does not prevent play.

**Reporting preferences:** Gameplay analytics is optional, starts on and can be turned off in Settings. A successfully saved opt-out stays off after an update. If Settings cannot save the choice, capture stays off for the current session; use Retry saving analytics choice before closing the app. The game includes no crash reporting.

**Samsung Gaming Hub:** Emberline declares itself as a game. If your installation is not automatically added, try Gaming Hub → My games → menu → Add games. Library inclusion does not guarantee every Game Booster feature. Include your One UI version and whether you installed from Google Play when reporting recognition problems.

Refunds are managed through the store where you purchased. Include the store/product name when asking for purchase support, but do not post receipts publicly.

Privacy policy: **[Read the privacy policy](privacy-policy.md)**. Publisher: **Mendoxe**.
