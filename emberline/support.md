# Emberline Support

Email 42dox.tester@gmail.com with your device model, operating-system version, app version shown in **Settings**, and what happened. Include the map and wave for a gameplay problem. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or private account keys, or post receipts publicly.

**Restore a purchase:** open **Settings → Restore purchases** while connected, using the same Apple or Google store account that purchased the content. Restore recovers ownership, not local saved progress. Apple purchases do not automatically restore on Android or vice versa. For purchase support, include the product name and purchase support ID shown in Settings, if available. Refunds are managed through the store where you purchased.

**Offline play:** available maps and saved runs work offline. Purchasing, restoring and connecting a platform game account need a connection. Previously verified ownership is cached for offline play and reconciled with the store when connectivity returns.

**Game accounts:** Google Play Games or Apple Game Center connection is optional through **Settings → Connect game account**. This initial release does not upload achievements or provide cloud saves. Local medals work without an account; connection failure does not prevent play.

**Analytics:** optional and off until you choose **Allow analytics**. Change your choice in **Settings → Gameplay analytics**. If an opt-out cannot be saved, collection stops for the current session; retry saving the choice before closing the game. Withdrawing consent stops future collection but does not automatically delete previously received events. Email support for access or deletion requests. The purchase support ID is separate from the analytics ID. The game has no crash-reporting service.

**Samsung Gaming Hub:** Emberline declares itself as a game. If it is not added automatically, try **Gaming Hub → My games → menu → Add games**. Inclusion does not guarantee every Game Booster feature. Include your One UI version and whether you installed from Google Play when reporting recognition problems.

See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) and [terms of use](terms-of-use.md). Emberline is made by an individual developer who publishes as mendox.
