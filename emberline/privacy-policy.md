# Emberline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Emberline is a tower defense game for iOS and Android. There is no Emberline account or developer-operated game server.

## Who is responsible

Emberline is made by an individual developer who publishes as mendox. The developer is responsible for the processing described here and can be reached at 42dox.tester@gmail.com.

## Data on your device

Emberline stores your progress, saved runs, medals, settings and cosmetic selections on your device. Full saves are not sent to the developer. If you allow analytics, limited gameplay summaries are sent as described below. Available maps and saved runs work offline.

Deleting the game or losing your device can remove local progress permanently; operating-system backups may behave differently. Restoring purchases restores ownership, not saved progress.

## Usage analytics

Usage analytics are off until you choose **Allow analytics**. Declining or dismissing the question leaves them off. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Gameplay analytics**. An earlier enabled preference requires a fresh choice; a saved opt-out remains off after updates.

If you allow analytics, Emberline sends PostHog, its analytics provider on servers in the EU, a limited set of gameplay milestones and shop actions. These include session and run starts, tutorial outcomes, campaign completions, defeats or abandoned runs, local achievement unlocks and purchase-flow events. Run summaries may include the map, challenge, wave, simulation duration, enemies defeated, core damage, remaining tower counts, upgrade branches and power use. Events also contain timestamps, event and run IDs, app version, platform, environment and a random installation ID created on your device. Your IP address reaches PostHog when your device connects to it.

These details are linked to the random installation ID, not your name. They help improve game balance and understand feature use and purchase flows. Analytics events are kept for up to one year and then deleted.

Emberline disables PostHog person profiles and IP-derived location enrichment. It does not send every tap, shot or enemy action, record your screen, use session replay or track you across apps for advertising. Pseudonymous data is not anonymous data. The game includes no crash-reporting service.

Up to 64 unsent events are kept on your device and expire after 72 hours. Turning analytics off stops future capture, cancels pending delivery and attempts to delete the local queue, installation ID and deduplication history. If local storage cannot be updated, cleanup is retried on a disabled startup. If the preference cannot be saved, capture still stops for the current session; Settings keeps showing the saved choice so you can retry before closing the game. Turning analytics off does not automatically delete events already received by PostHog.

## Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments through its store. Emberline uses RevenueCat to verify ownership and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives a random customer ID, store transaction information and purchase history, including product, price, currency and purchase date, along with SDK app, device and network information. RevenueCat also provides purchase reporting to the developer. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

The purchase service starts when a configured game installation opens, independently of analytics consent, so it can check existing ownership and load prices even if you have not bought anything. Purchase processing cannot be switched off with the analytics setting.

Purchase and restoration requests need a connection. Verified ownership is cached on your device for offline use and reconciled with the store when connectivity returns, including refunds. Purchases use the relevant store account and do not automatically transfer between Apple and Android. Purchase providers and stores retain records as needed for restoration, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

## Optional game accounts

You can choose to connect Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. The platform handles authentication, and Emberline stores the platform player ID locally to remember the connection. Platform account information is handled under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. This initial release does not upload achievements, provide cloud saves or transfer purchases between stores. Local medals work without a connection.

## Support emails

If you email support, your address and message are stored in the developer's email account, provided by Google, and are kept only as long as needed to answer you. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details or private account keys.

## Why this data is used

Under the GDPR, the legal bases are:

- **Consent** for optional gameplay analytics.
- **Performing a contract** for purchases, restoring them, requested platform-account connection and answering support requests about these services.
- **Legitimate interests** in answering other support requests.
- **Legal obligations** for records the law requires the developer to keep.

Analytics and platform-account connection are optional. Purchase data is needed to verify and restore ownership.

## Service providers and transfers

- **PostHog** processes optional gameplay analytics on the developer's behalf, on servers in the EU.
- **RevenueCat**, based in the United States, processes purchase data on the developer's behalf.
- **Apple and Google** handle store purchases and optional game accounts under their own terms and privacy policies.
- **Google** provides the support mailbox.

The developer does not sell your personal data. When data leaves the EU, it is protected by safeguards the law allows, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Choosing PostHog's EU project does not mean all providers and subprocessors process data only in the EU.

Provider information: [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy), [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, and receive a copy of it. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time without affecting processing before withdrawal. Email 42dox.tester@gmail.com for these requests or information about retention and transfer safeguards.

Because Emberline has no accounts, the developer cannot locate your analytics events from your name or email alone. Turning analytics off removes the local analytics ID when storage is writable and can prevent the developer from locating earlier events. Contact support for help identifying relevant records; do not send sensitive documents unnecessarily. For purchase support, the purchase support ID shown in Settings can help locate RevenueCat records. It is separate from the analytics ID. Store and platform-account requests may need to go to Apple or Google; legal retention requirements may limit deletion.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Czechia, that is the [Office for Personal Data Protection](https://uoou.gov.cz).

## Children

Emberline is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the developer so it can be investigated.
