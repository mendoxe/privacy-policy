# Emberline privacy policy

Publisher: **Mendoxe**
Contact: **[42dox.tester@gmail.com](mailto:42dox.tester@gmail.com)**
Last updated: **10 November 2026**

This policy covers the Emberline game.

## Playing Emberline

Emberline stores game progress, saved runs, medals, settings and cosmetic selections on your device. You can play available maps offline. The game does not require an Emberline account, contain advertising, or use advertising identifiers. It does not record your screen or gameplay video.

Removing the app generally removes local progress; operating-system backups may behave differently. Restoring purchases restores ownership, not campaign progress. Platform achievements are separate from local saves.

## Purchases

If you buy permanent campaign content or skins, Apple or Google processes the payment. We do not receive your full payment-card details. RevenueCat verifies purchases and manages ownership using store transaction information and an app installation/customer identifier. Purchase and restoration requests require a connection. Verified permanent ownership is cached securely on your device so temporary loss of connectivity does not remove your content; ownership is reconciled with verified store information when connectivity returns, including refunds.

RevenueCat, Apple and Google process information according to their applicable policies. Restoring purchases uses the relevant store account. Purchases are not automatically transferable between Apple and Android.

## Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay analytics is enabled by default and can be disabled or enabled in Settings. A successfully saved opt-out remains off after an update. When enabled in a configured store build, PostHog receives a small set of events such as session/run starts, campaign completions, defeats, introduction completion, local achievement unlocks and purchase-screen actions. Run summaries may include map, challenge, wave reached, game duration, enemies defeated, core damage, remaining tower counts, upgrade branches and power-up use counts, together with app/platform information and a random pseudonymous installation identifier. Events are sent through PostHog's HTTPS capture API to its EU Cloud project. We disable person profiles and enrichment of events with IP-derived location. The provider still receives ordinary network/request information, including the source IP. This helps us understand difficulty, reliability of purchase flows and which features players use.

We do not collect every tap, shot or enemy action, use analytics for cross-app advertising, or enable session replay. A pseudonymous identifier is not the same as anonymous data. At most 64 unsent events are stored locally, and they expire after 72 hours. Turning analytics off stops future capture, cancels pending uploads and removes the local outbox, installation identifier and deduplication state when device storage is writable. If saving your preference fails, capture stays off for the current session and Settings asks you to retry before closing the app. A request already received by the service cannot be withdrawn by cancelling the client. Previously received data is subject to the service's retention and deletion process.

## Diagnostics

Emberline includes no crash-reporting service.

## Game accounts and local medals

You can optionally connect a Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account. Authentication is handled by that platform, and the game stores the platform player identifier locally to remember the connection. Local medals work independently. This release does not upload earned achievements to those platforms. Platform account data is governed by Google's or Apple's policies. Connecting does not enable cloud saves or purchase transfers between stores.

## Sharing, retention and your choices

Data is processed by the active providers named above to deliver purchases, optional analytics and game-account authentication. We do not sell your personal data. Local progress and settings remain until removed by you or your operating system. Received analytics is kept under the PostHog project's retention and deletion settings; the 72-hour local queue limit does not apply to data already delivered. Purchase providers and stores may retain transaction records for purchase restoration, fraud prevention and their legal obligations. Support email and information you choose to send are used to answer your request and follow up on the issue.

Providers may process information in countries outside your own under their applicable terms and data-processing safeguards. PostHog's selected project is EU Cloud; this does not mean all providers and subprocessors process all data solely in the EU. Their privacy and data-processing information is linked below. Contact us for information about current retention or to request deletion.

Use Settings to change optional analytics preferences, restore purchases, view this policy and contact support. For access/deletion requests concerning received analytics, contact the publisher above. We will explain what information is needed to locate the relevant data without unnecessarily collecting identifying information. Store transaction records and platform account data may require requests through the respective platform and may have retention requirements. Your rights depend on your location and applicable law.

Emberline is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The policy shows its last updated date.
