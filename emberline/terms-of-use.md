## Purchases

Eight maps, all five towers and their upgrade branches, earnable powers, three loadout slots, speed controls, saves and endless play on available maps are free without usage limits. Full Campaign is a one-time purchase that permanently unlocks twelve more maps and Sunforged skins. Arctic and Neon are separate one-time cosmetic purchases. Skins do not change combat statistics. There are no ads, consumables or subscriptions.

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. The store shows the price and what the purchase includes before you pay, and its terms apply to the payment. A purchase is tied to your store account. Use **Settings → Restore purchases** while connected with the same store account to restore ownership on a new device. Restoring does not restore local progress or automatically transfer purchases between Apple and Android.

Verified ownership is cached for offline play and checked again with the store when connectivity returns. A refund can remove access to purchased content; it does not delete your existing saved runs. Apple and Google handle refunds under their own rules: [Apple refunds](https://support.apple.com/118223), [Google Play refunds](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).
